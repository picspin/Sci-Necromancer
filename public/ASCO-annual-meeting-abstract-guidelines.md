## Supported pathways

- Regular Abstract: Background, Methods, Results, Conclusions.
- Late-Breaking Shell: Background, Methods, primary endpoint, analysis type, data-freeze date, planned statistics, and expected final-data date.
- Late-Breaking Final: Background, Methods, Results, Conclusions.
- Trials in Progress: Background and Methods only; no results or preliminary outcome data.

## Core constraints

- Title + body + table: at most 2,600 characters excluding spaces.
- At most one table with no more than 10 rows; no figures.
- Objective title without results or conclusions.
- Generic drug names; no case reports.
- Use an official track and subcategory. ASCO assigns the presentation format.
- Verify statistical methods, trial registration, ethics/IRB, consent, funding, disclosures, prior presentation, and embargo requirements where applicable.
- Never infer or invent missing study data.
