# ESMO Congress 2026 abstract rule baseline

Official regulation:

- https://dam.esmo.org/image/upload/v1770284726/ESMO-Congress-2026-Abstract-Regulations_ahdmjx.pdf
- https://www.esmo.org/meeting-calendar/esmo-congress-2026

## Supported pathways

- Regular Abstract: Background, Methods, Results, Conclusions.
- Late-Breaking Preliminary Intent: title, author/contact and declaration details, Background, Methods, expected Results, and expected Conclusions.
- Late-Breaking Final: final author list, Results, Conclusions, and optional compliant table.
- Trial in Progress: Background and Trial design only; no results or preliminary data; Poster/ePoster only.

## Core constraints

- Title + body + table: at most 2,000 characters excluding spaces.
- At most one table; it counts as 225 characters by default and must not exceed 600 characters.
- No illustrations or graphs; at most 20 authors; no case reports.
- Do not state results or conclusions in the title. Do not use commercial drug names in the title.
- Define abbreviations on first use and preserve patient anonymity.
- Verify trial registration/protocol, approval, consent, funding, governing legal entity, editorial assistance, prior presentation, embargo, and declarations of interest.
- For clinical/translational patient data, check presenter independence from the sponsor/company under the official exceptions.
- Presentation format is a recommendation; the ESMO Scientific Committee makes the final decision.

## Generative AI

- AI may assist an initial draft only with human oversight.
- Authors retain full responsibility; AI cannot be an author or cited as an author.
- If AI generated or analysed research data, identify the model/tool and its use in Methods.
- Basic spelling, grammar, and reference-formatting assistance does not require the same declaration.

AI-assisted text may be inaccurate and does not guarantee eligibility, compliance, acceptance, presentation, or publication.
